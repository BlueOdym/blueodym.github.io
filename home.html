About me

For me, 3D has always been a passion. Animated films and video games showed me worlds that seemed impossible, yet somehow real, and left me with the feeling that anything I could imagine could be created. That spark of wonder became curiosity, and curiosity became obsession. I spent countless hours watching timelapses, breakdowns, and behind-the-scenes videos, trying to understand how these worlds were built. Eventually, all of that curiosity led to one simple question: why couldn't I be one of the artists creating these worlds?

Two years later, with my high school diploma in hand, I was faced with another question: could I make a living from this passion? The answer came almost instantly, and with it a sense of certainty. I decided to give it everything I had. I then pursued a Bachelor's degree at Paris Ynov Campus. Those years were some of the most enjoyable and formative of my life, but they also made me realize how much further I wanted to push myself. I was eager to grow in a more demanding environment, surrounded by artists who shared the same ambition and dedication. That desire led me to set my sights on New3dge, ranked among the world's leading creative schools by The Rookies. Today, I am pursuing a Master's degree there, continuing to refine my artistic and technical skills while striving to reach the highest standards of the industry.

I work every day with the same determination that first drew me to 3D, always striving to improve my skills and move closer to my goals. Curiosity has always been one of my greatest strengths. While 3D remains my primary focus, I am deeply interested in a wide range of creative and technical fields, including technical art, web design, and photography. Exploring disciplines beyond 3D allows me to broaden my perspective and continuously discover new ways to create and solve problems.

Darth Maul

I did the blocking in Blender and went into ZBrush for the sculpting, then textured in Substance Painter and rendered in Blender. Darth Maul is a character I really enjoyed making and my big revelation in character art.

I've maintained him and consider it my test subject for a lot of my experiments.

Especially giving him the look of Jack Gyllenhaal in Demolition (2015)

TMNT Furinkazan

Made for my second semestral project at New3dge.

TMNT Mikey Furinkazan from BigBoysToys by Tik-Ka From East Made with 3 Albedo 4k UDIM textures (Albedo) and two 4k shared Bump and Roughness maps.

All the sculpting has been done in Zbrush and hard surface in 3ds Max, with all of the renders with Vray in 3ds Max. I will maybe add some with Cycles in Blender later.

Bryan Cranston

I wanted to work on a face, as I did not have the time or necessity to do it on Darth Maul.

So I choose Bryan Cranston as a friend was doing it too, the wrinkles and skin texture interested me.

I've since done a partial rework, doing a lot of work on the skin details using xyz textures and JHill Skin details alpha pack. I should redo it from the start to train and get the overall much more closer to the actual model

Far West Environment

One of my assignment at New3dge.

We had to do replicate in 3D the concept art we had done just before.

It was really fun to do (though I had uncertainties in the texturing phase), especially the compositing phase where you can really level up the overall quality of your image. We have worked with multi-layers .EXR files which is always a blast, it's so practical to use.

I'm not entirely happy with the modeling and the composition could be better, but I'm proud of the result nonetheless.
